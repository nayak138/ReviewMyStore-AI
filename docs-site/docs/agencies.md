# 5-Star.AI for Agencies

This guide explains how an agency can set up client businesses, assign the
right access, and run review, feedback, campaign, and social workflows in
5-Star.AI.

The workspace is agency-led. Agency owners can manage the organization;
invited teammates only get the businesses and feature permissions explicitly
assigned to them.

## 1. Understand the workspace and roles

**OWNER** is the organization owner. Owners can see the organization’s
businesses, manage business records and teams, and use owner-level workflows.

**TEAM_MEMBER** is an invited teammate. A teammate sees only active businesses
assigned to them and only the features granted for each business. Archived or
suspended businesses are not available to teammates.

There is no separate store-owner account type in the current app. A client
operator uses the access provisioned by the agency owner; access is not granted
just because someone knows a business URL.

## 2. Set up the agency portfolio

Sign in through the agency workspace or accept the invitation sent by the
agency owner.

Open **Businesses**. An owner can add a business by finding the correct Google
Places listing, then confirming the name, address, and location. Each business
record is associated with one location.

Check that the business is active and that the Google Business Profile belongs
to the intended client and location. Adding a business record and authorizing
Google are separate steps.

For social publishing, connect the appropriate provider accounts and select
the intended Facebook Page, Instagram account, or Threads profile for each
business.

## 3. Assign teammate access by business

Only an **OWNER** can manage business teams and invitations from **Settings**.
Assign a teammate to the specific client businesses they need, then choose
the narrowest useful permission for each feature.

**Campaigns**, **Review Inbox**, **Feedback**, and **Social Media** each support
**NONE**, **VIEW**, or **MANAGE** per business. VIEW is for reading; MANAGE is
needed for actions such as creating, editing, connecting, or publishing.

**Analytics** supports NONE or VIEW only for teammates. Do not grant or promise
Analytics management access; organization-level Insights remain an
owner-level view.

Owners can see the organization’s businesses, including archived businesses.
Team members are limited to assigned, active businesses. Server-side checks
decide whether an action is allowed.

If a teammate cannot open a feature or complete an action, check their
assignment and that feature’s permission before changing account connections.

## 4. Run a campaign and share its review page

Open the selected business’s **Campaigns** tab and create a campaign from a
template or from scratch.

Set a clear campaign name and add relevant keywords or talking points. Review
the generated campaign content before sharing it.

Open **QR Codes** to copy the campaign link or download its QR code. Share the
link or display the QR where customers can reach it.

Customers open the campaign in a web browser; they do not need to install a
5-Star.AI app. The public flow helps a customer reflect on highlights, draft
and edit their own review, then copy it to Google. It does not submit a Google
review automatically.

## 5. Manage Google reviews

Open **Review Inbox** for the client business, connect the Google account if
needed, and choose the correct Google Business location.

Sync reviews, then search or filter the inbox to find reviews that need
attention.

Generate an AI reply draft when useful. A person should review and edit it;
publishing requires an explicit confirmation and is not automatic.

Sensitive or low-rating reviews can require approval. Read the warning and
confirm the final reply before publishing it to Google.

## 6. Work with private feedback and analytics

Use **Feedback** to review customer feedback submitted through the business
experience. Treat it as an internal service-recovery workflow, separate from
public Google reviews.

Use business **Analytics** for review, customer-feedback, and campaign
activity for one business. Use **Insights** for the agency-level view across
businesses.

A teammate with Analytics VIEW can inspect analytics but cannot be given
Analytics MANAGE permission.

## 7. Publish social content and handle comments

Open **Social Media** for the business and connect the required provider
account. Select the exact available Page or profile the business should
publish from.

Create a post, choose its channel or channels, add text and supported media,
then publish now or schedule it.

Import public comments for an eligible post and reply from the reply desk when
a response is appropriate.

Before switching an account, verify the replacement target. Reconnecting or
switching provider accounts does not reset the business’s usage ledger.

## 8. Usage, provider capacity, and operating boundaries

Business-level app allowances and external provider capacity are separate.
Provider capacity can be shared across businesses, so available provider
capacity may be lower than a business’s remaining app allowance. Usage windows
and publishing requirements are explained in the
[Usage & Social Publishing Guide](/usage-and-social).

Do not promise unlimited reviews, AI actions, or social publishing. Check
current usage and provider status when an operation is unavailable.

The app supports reputation campaigns, reviews, private feedback, analytics,
social publishing, and comment replies. It does not replace Google or
social-network account access; each provider connection and target must be
authorized separately.

## 9. Agency operating checklist

- Confirm the correct business and location before connecting a provider or
  importing data.
- Assign team members per business and per feature; review access when
  responsibilities change.
- Review AI-generated customer-facing content before it is copied or
  published.
- Use the [usage guide](/usage-and-social) when an app allowance differs from
  provider capacity or a publish/import is blocked.
