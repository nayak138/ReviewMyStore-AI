# Sprint 3 – Smart Business Discovery, Campaign Management & AI Review Flow

## Objective

Sprint 3 should transform ReviewMyStore.ai from a dashboard application into a working MVP.

The outcome of this sprint should be that a business owner can:

1. Find their business using Google Search.
2. Create and manage Campaigns.
3. Configure Keywords.
4. Share a Review Page.
5. Generate an AI-powered Google Review.
6. Copy the review.
7. Redirect customers to the official Google Review page.

Do NOT implement QR Analytics, NFC Analytics, Orders, Billing, Subscriptions or Super Admin enhancements in this sprint.

# 2. Smart Business Discovery

Replace manual business creation with a smarter onboarding flow.

Landing Page should include:

"Find Your Business"

Search input using Google Places Autocomplete.

When a business is selected:

Retrieve:

- Business Name
- Address
- Category
- Google Place ID
- Website
- Phone Number
- Google Rating
- Review Count
- Primary Business Image (if available)

Temporarily store this information until registration is complete.

---

# 8. AI Review Generation

Continue using the AI Service abstraction.

Never call the AI provider directly.

Generate:

- Natural sounding review
- 2–3 sentences
- Human tone
- No emojis
- No hashtags
- No fake claims
- Never mention AI

Allow:

- Generate
- Edit
- Regenerate (Maximum 3)

---

# 9. Copy & Redirect

Primary Button:

Copy & Post to Google

Workflow:

1. Copy review to clipboard.
2. Show success toast.
3. Redirect to:

https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid={google_place_id}

Must work reliably on desktop and mobile browsers.
