---
name: Landing marketing claim boundaries
description: User-required no-sign-up wording and evidence limits for the public product demo.
---

The homepage must say “No Sign Up” alongside “7-Day Free Trial” and “No Credit Card,” but qualify that this means no 5-Star.AI account creation to request a guided trial. It does not mean no contact form, instant anonymous dashboard access, or bypassing Google's own sign-in.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested that reassurance while preserving the guided request flow and customer's control of Google publishing. Treating the label as a provisioning requirement would introduce unauthorized product scope.

**How to apply:** Keep the qualifier near trial calls to action and in the FAQ. Do not change authentication or trial provisioning to match an unqualified marketing slogan.

The existing named hotel demonstration is not customer endorsement evidence. Business-preview fixtures and demonstration actions must remain separate from published customer proof.

**Why:** The landing brief requires genuine evidence, and the supplied demonstration includes a real external review destination. Visual credibility is not permission to claim endorsement or post a review.

**How to apply:** Require approval and sourcing before publishing testimonials/results; label illustrative content and intercept external publishing during tests.

Fictional feedback is permitted as a visual product example when it is visibly identified as illustrative, not as customer proof.

**Why:** The user explicitly invited imaginary feedback while requesting richer homepage graphics. That permission does not establish real endorsements, customer identities, or business results.

**How to apply:** Keep the fictional label next to the cards, use fictional example identities, and never add “Verified” badges or testimonial claims to those examples.

The free guided trial must not be represented as a permanently free product or a public paid price, including machine-readable metadata.

**Why:** Commercial approval is for a free request-led trial followed only by an accepted written custom quote. A generic zero-price software offer tells crawlers a different story from the visible pricing policy.

**How to apply:** Apply the same quote/trial distinction to structured data, social previews, and crawler summaries as to visible pricing copy.
